Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Platform Manager ("the App", "we", "us"), available at https://video-manager-nine.vercel.app/, collects, uses and protects information when you use the App.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information from connected platforms. When you connect a TikTok account, we receive basic profile information permitted by the user.info.basic scope (such as your display name, avatar and a unique user ID).
  • Access tokens. We store the access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok and other platforms so the App can publish on your behalf.
  • Content you upload. Videos, captions and settings you provide for publishing.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to show which accounts are connected and to publish the content you choose to those accounts, using the video.publish scope for TikTok. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.

3. Sharing

We do not sell or rent your information. Your content is sent only to the platforms you choose to publish to (such as TikTok). We use hosting providers (such as Vercel) to run the App, and they process data only to provide that service.

4. Storage and security

Tokens and content are stored securely and kept only as long as needed to provide the service. Uploaded videos are deleted after publishing is complete or when you delete them.

5. Your choices and deletion

You can disconnect any account at any time in the App. For TikTok, you can also revoke access in the TikTok app under Settings → Security and permissions → Apps and services. When you disconnect an account, we delete its stored tokens. To request deletion of all your data, email us at the address below.

6. Children

The App is not intended for anyone under 18.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above shows when it was last changed.
